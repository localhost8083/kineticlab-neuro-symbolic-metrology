# Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon

**Hosts:** Meta & Cerebral Valley  
**Format:** Virtual / Remote (Worldwide)  
**Dates:** October 26 at 12:00 PM – November 4 at 9:00 PM EST  
**Prize Pool:** $1,000,000 Total Cash Prizes  

---

## Overview

Ten days, access to Meta’s newest models, and $1,000,000 in cash prizes. The Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon is virtual, worldwide, and free to enter if selected.

Bring a problem you know firsthand, or an idea that feels out of reach. Build it with the models, learn from the Meta team and partners in live workshops, and compete for a share in the $1,000,000 cash prize pool across two tracks: one for indie developers and one for startups.

## Participant Tracks

Every submitted project must enter **one** of the two tracks:

### 1. Indie Developer Track
* **Target Audience:** For individual builders, students, and small indie teams (up to 4 members).
* **Evaluation Criteria:** Judged on creativity, technical ambition, and functional execution (whether it works).

### 2. Startup Track
* **Target Audience:** For technical founders, existing teams, and startups building a real product.
* **Evaluation Criteria:** Judged on technical execution, production readiness, and viability under real-world load.

---

## Project Themes

Regardless of your chosen track, projects should align with one of two main themes:

### Theme 1: Build for Your World
Bring Meta’s models into your specific domain. Solve a problem you experience firsthand, automate workflows to free up time and mental bandwidth, or create a solution that was previously impossible.

### Theme 2: Push the Frontier
Leverage Meta’s models to tackle complex challenges in software, robotics, manufacturing, energy, materials, and physical systems. Grant machines new capabilities, accelerate scientific experimentation, or unlock deeper real-world understanding and control.

## Judging Criteria

Submissions will be evaluated by a panel of judges from Meta, Cerebral Valley, and partner organizations:

1. **Impact (30%):** Does the project address a meaningful problem or present a transformative opportunity?
2. **Technical Execution (25%):** Depth of engineering, code quality, stability, and handling of complex logic.
3. **Use of Meta AI Tooling (25%):** Effective integration and utilization of Meta’s model APIs or open-weights ecosystem.
4. **Demo & Presentation (20%):** Quality of the live working demo, documentation, and video submission.

## Application & Eligibility Details

* **Capacity Limit:** Capped at **20,000 accepted participants**.
* **Review Process:** Rolling basis. The application portal closes once the 20,000-builder cap is met, even if prior to the Oct 25 deadline.
* **Eligibility:** Open globally to developers aged 18+ (excluding standard sanctioned regions).
* **Submission Requirements:** Functional demo, public code repository link, and a short video demonstration submitted via Cerebral Valley before Nov 4, 9:00 PM EST.
